Privacy & accessibility

A clear, simple approach to this website preview.

Your information

This website has no accounts, analytics or online application database. The enquiry form prepares an email in your own email application. It does not transmit or store your entries on a school server. Your email provider handles the email when you choose to send it.

External services

Directions open Google Maps. Typography may be requested from Google Fonts; system fonts are used if it is unavailable. These external services receive the normal connection information required to serve their content and have their own privacy terms.

Accessible by design

The site supports keyboard navigation, visible focus outlines, responsive layouts and reduced motion preferences. If you encounter a barrier, email the school with a description of the issue.

School content

This is a website concept. The name, address and email come from the supplied school document; the crest and its motto come from the supplied project asset. Proposed wording and unavailable details are marked. Official policies and school-specific information require school approval.
